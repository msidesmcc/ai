About Mary Sides

Teaching the Next Generation of Scientists

Hello and welcome to my academic hub! As a Professor of Biology at McLennan Community College in Waco, Texas, my objective is to clarify the natural world for my students and provide them with the robust mental tools needed for academic and clinical careers.

My instruction combines active lecturing, real-world case studies, and engaging laboratory practices. Whether you are aiming for nursing, planning to transfer to a four-year institution, or fulfilling core requirements, you will find resources here designed to support your journey.
